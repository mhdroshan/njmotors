Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about non-RTO electric scooters and driving regulations in India.

Is it truly legal to ride without a driving license? ▼
Yes, absolutely. Under the Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR) of India, electric two-wheelers with a maximum speed capped at 25 km/h and a motor capacity of 250W or less are officially classified as non-motorized vehicles / low-speed electric bicycles. They are legally exempt from requiring a driving license, vehicle registration, road tax, and PUC certification.
Can school and college students ride these scooters? ▼
Yes! Because no driving license is required, students aged 14 and above can comfortably commute to school, college, tuition centers, and sports practices without any legal hassle.
How do I charge the battery at home? ▼
All Urban Bikes models can be charged using any regular 3-pin 5A or 15A wall socket in your home, apartment parking, or office. Most models also feature a portable detachable lithium battery pack that you can carry upstairs with the included handle.
What warranty comes with Urban Bikes electric scooters? ▼
All Lithium-ion models come with a 3-Year Comprehensive Battery Warranty and 1-Year Motor & Controller Warranty directly backed by NJ Motors dealership.
How does Doorstep Home Delivery work? ▼
Once you choose your favorite Urban eBikes model and color (via WhatsApp or website), our team performs a complete Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) and dispatches the scooter safely to your doorstep. Upon arrival, our technician walks you through key features, demonstrates home charging, and hands over your keys and warranty documents.

Yes! NJ Motors provides convenient Doorstep Home Service. Our certified EV technicians can visit your home address for periodic maintenance checkups, battery health testing, brake & electrical inspections, and genuine spare parts replacement without you having to travel to the showroom.
